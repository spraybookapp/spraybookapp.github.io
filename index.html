Spraybook

A personal, shareable book of your spray-wall problems.

Photograph your wall once, circle holds to build routes, log your sends, and lend walls or routes to friends. No account, no sign-in, works offline. Everything stays on your phone.

Getting help

Email odelia.labs@protonmail.com. Please say which phone you are using and what you were doing when the problem happened — a screenshot helps more than anything else.

Common questions

How do I build a route?
Choose Start (green), Hold (blue) or Finish (red), then tap the wall photo to place a marker. Every route needs at least one start and one finish. Tap a marker to select it, then drag it, or use the controls to resize, rotate, nudge, recolour or delete it. Pinch to zoom, drag with one finger to move around, and double-tap to fit the whole wall again.
Someone sent me a wall. How do I open it?
Save the file to your phone first — you cannot import it straight out of a message. On an iPhone, tap the file, then Share, then Save to Files. On Android, tap the file and save it; it usually lands in Downloads. Then open Spraybook, tap Import on the walls screen, and pick the file. On an iPhone there is also a shortcut: tap the file, then Share, then Spraybook.
I already have that wall. Will importing duplicate it?
No. Spraybook notices and offers to merge instead, which keeps your own routes and adds theirs to the same wall. Importing never changes your attempts and sends.
Will I lose my routes when the app updates?
No. Updating replaces the app, not your data. You only lose everything if the app is deleted — so back up before changing phones.
How do I back up?
Settings → Back up library, then send the file somewhere off your phone. Everything lives on your device and there is no copy on a server, so a backup is the only way to recover your walls if the phone is lost. Unlike sharing, a backup does include your attempts and sends.
Does my friend see my sends when I share a wall?
No. Sharing includes the wall and its routes only. Your climbing record stays private.

Known limitations

  • On Android, a .spraybook file cannot be opened straight into the app — Android has no way to associate a private file type with an app. Save the file, then use Import.
  • Spraybook is designed for phones in portrait, and is not optimised for tablets.
  • There is no cloud sync. Your data lives on one device, by design.
