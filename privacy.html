Privacy Policy

Spraybook · Last updated 15 August 2026

The short version: Spraybook does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Your walls, photos, routes and climbing history stay on your phone unless you choose to share or back them up yourself.

What Spraybook stores

Everything you create — wall photos, routes, hold positions, grades, setter names and your record of attempts and sends — is stored in a database on your own device. None of it is transmitted anywhere. The developer cannot see it, and has no way to retrieve it.

Camera and photo library

Spraybook asks for camera access so you can photograph a wall, and photo library access so you can choose an existing picture instead. Photos you select are copied into the app's own storage on your device. Spraybook does not read, index or upload anything else from your library, and does not use photographs for any purpose other than displaying the wall you chose.

Sharing and backups

When you tap Share, Spraybook creates a .spraybook file and hands it to your phone's normal sharing screen. You choose where it goes. The file contains the wall photo and the routes on it. It does not include your attempts or sends — your climbing record stays private when you share a wall with someone.

A backup file, created from Settings, does include your attempts and sends, because its purpose is to restore your own history. Treat a backup as personal, and store it wherever you would keep any other personal file.

Once you send a file to someone, it is out of Spraybook's hands and governed by whatever service you used to send it.

No analytics, tracking or advertising

Spraybook contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party software development kits that collect information about you. It makes no network requests of its own.

Information Apple and Google may collect

Spraybook is distributed through the App Store and, where applicable, Google Play. Those platforms may collect information about downloads and crashes according to their own policies and your device settings. If you have opted in to sharing crash and usage data with developers, the developer may see aggregated, anonymous reports. This is handled entirely by Apple and Google — Spraybook itself sends them nothing.

Children

Spraybook is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing held elsewhere to request or erase. Settings also has a Reset app data option if you want to clear everything while keeping the app installed. Neither can be undone, so make a backup first if you might want your routes later.

Changes to this policy

If Spraybook ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here and the date at the top changed. If a future version were to collect anything, that would be stated plainly and asked for clearly.
